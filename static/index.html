METHOD & SOURCES

How to read this engine

01

Observed, then compared

Prices come from Yahoo Finance through yfinance. Event dates come from the FRED CPIAUCSL series and are treated as observation-release proxies.

02

Trading-day aware

Event windows snap to the nearest available market observation. Returns, volatility, drawdown and correlations are calculated from the retrieved close series.

03

No hindsight dressing

Decision Replay is a historical hypothetical. It does not claim an alternative allocation was knowable in advance, and historical performance is not a guarantee of future returns.
